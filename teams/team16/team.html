Meet the team

The people behind Predicting NYC Taxi Trip Duration through Spatial Network and Regression Analysis.

Arjun Gangwar

IS24BM014, Department of Mathematics

Built the regression pipeline from scratch in NumPy — Ordinary Least Squares via the normal equation, Gradient Descent with a provably safe step size, and Ridge regression with 5-fold cross-validated α.

Anant Tripathi

MC24BT001, Department of Mathematics

Handled data cleaning and exploratory analysis — filtering impossible trips, diagnosing the skewed trip-duration distribution, and establishing the log-transform that made the target fit for linear regression.

Trisham Bepari

CS24BT022, Department of Computer Science

Engineered the 16 geometric, temporal and interaction features — Haversine and Manhattan distance, bearing, cyclical hour/day encoding, and the rush-hour and weekend interaction terms.

Arnav Raju Sale

MC24BT021, Department of Mathematics

Built the spatial network — the 50-zone K-means graph, the symmetric graph Laplacian with its positive-semi-definiteness proof, spectral clustering into 5 neighbourhoods, and the four centrality scores.

Chahat Choudhary

MC24BT027, Department of Mathematics

Ran the statistical validation — Welch's t-tests comparing models, the 200-iteration bootstrap for coefficient confidence intervals, condition-number analysis, and the four-way feature-set ablation study.
