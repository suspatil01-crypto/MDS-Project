Meet the team

Five students from the Department of CSE, IIT Dharwad, for CS407T Mathematics for Data Science.

Naveen T S

CS24BT006, Computer Science and Engineering

Contributed in game endings and blunder analysis. Classified how games end by speed and rating, and built the move-15 time-forfeit model (ROC AUC 0.747). Compared two blunder definitions, mapped where errors occur by phase, and built the next-move blunder model (ROC AUC 0.787).

Jami Sai Harshit

CS24BT026, Computer Science and Engineering

Contributed in time allocation by phase. Measured how thinking time is split across opening, middlegame and endgame, and how much clock is left after moves 15 and 30. Clustered 562,687 player-games into two time-management styles and checked them against time-forfeit rates.

Bhuvan Saraf

CS24BT028, Computer Science and Engineering

Contributed in openings in rating mismatches. Built a binomial model of the underdog's expected score from rating gap, rating, speed and colour, then ranked about 300 opening families by their adjusted score lift with conservative confidence bounds.

Prakhar Gupta

CS24BT003, Computer Science and Engineering

Contributed in time pressure and accuracy. Compared accuracy under High and Low clock pressure within the same game, then fitted a weighted least-squares model with game-clustered errors to show how the pressure penalty grows with rating.

Kratagya Jain

CS24BT025, Computer Science and Engineering

Contributed in data exploration and preprocessing. Split the 708,978-game PGN by speed, then streamed it into game and move tables (672,718 games, 46.7 million moves) with clock fraction, thinking time, accuracy and blunder flags, plus validation checks.
